About

"I don't chase decisive moments. I wait for the ordinary ones to admit what they're really about."

Light Shutter Pic Photography has spent the last fourteen years photographing the seam between public and private life — markets before opening, apartment stairwells, the last hour of a wedding after the band has gone home. The work is shot almost entirely on black-and-white film, developed by hand, because the delay forces a kind of honesty: by the time the roll comes back, memory has already started editing itself, and the negatives get the final word.

Recent long-form projects have followed dockworkers along the Tagus, a barbershop in Naples that's changed owners four times but never its chairs, and a decade of the same New Year's Eve rooftop in Porto.
